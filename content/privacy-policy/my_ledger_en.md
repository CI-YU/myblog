Thank you for using "My Ledger". This Privacy Policy explains how this application handles your data.

---

### 1. Data Collection

"My Ledger" **does not collect any personal data**. This application does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

---

### 2. Data Storage

All accounting data you enter in the application is stored in a SQLite database on your local device.

- Data is **never uploaded to any server**
- Data is **never shared with third parties**
- Your data is entirely under your control and exists only on your device

---

### 3. File Import

This application provides a file import feature that allows you to import external data into the app.

- Imported file content is processed locally only
- No imported data is transmitted externally
- Imported data is stored in the local SQLite database

---

### 4. Network Access

"My Ledger" **does not require an internet connection** and does not perform any network transmission.

---

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

- **Deleting individual records**: Delete directly within the application
- **Deleting all data**: Clear the app data in your device settings, or uninstall the application

Upon uninstallation, all local data will be permanently deleted.

---

### 6. Data Security

This application takes the following measures to protect your data:

- All data is stored locally and never transmitted over a network, eliminating the risk of external access
- Database access is protected by the operating system's sandbox mechanism, preventing other applications from accessing your data

---

### 7. Third-Party Services

This application **does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services**.

---

### 8. Children's Privacy

This application is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

---

### 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, the content and date on this page will be updated. We recommend checking this page periodically.
